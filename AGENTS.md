# Rules for agents (Codex / ChatGPT and others)

Follow every rule in `CLAUDE.md` in this folder; it applies to all agents, not only Claude. In particular:
- `Task list.md`: tick your own points (✅ / ⚠️ / ❌ + short reason) as you go, and keep your
  `⏳ HH:MM <agent / task> — <doing now>` heartbeat line fresh at least every 15 minutes.
- No commit, push or deploy unless the user asks in that message.
- Never print secrets from `.env`. No Co-Authored-By or other AI attribution in commits.
